Welcome to Hotel Intourist

About Hotel Intourist Palace

Since 2019, Hotel Intourist Palace has provided unmatched hospitality in the heart of Itahari. With over 20 years of experience in the hotel and restaurant industry, we offer comfort, luxury, and a seamless experience to every guest. Located at Sangit Chowk, Itahari, our hotel is an ideal base for exploring Eastern Nepal.

Our Mission

To ensure a memorable and luxurious stay for our guests through top-class amenities, personalized services, and a welcoming environment. We aim to elevate both business and leisure travel experiences.
